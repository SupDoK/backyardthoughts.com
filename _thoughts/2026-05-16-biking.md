I hate biking with my whole soul. This is not about exercise. Let me explain.

The country that I am in right now, LOVES biking. I see bikes everywhere, everyone bikes anywhere, for sport, for pleasure, for commuting. I just can't wrap my head around the action. I love walking and I think is enough. I am totally comfortable taking the public transport. Why do I have to bike? 

It took me 5 years to buy a bike, because I really didn't need it. I bought it just because it would have been free (thank you company). Every time I say I don't like biking, people judge me for a second and asks if I can even ride one. Uhm YES? I just prefer not to. Why does it affect people so much? 

I have been recently diagnosed with Asthma too, leave-me-alone. I might be exaggerating, I know, but I just don't like it. Walking 20km? I am down. Biking for 15 minutes? I will not listen even. 

I am just a girl that likes walking or being in a bus, with music in her ears and a lot of daydreaming, let me be.
