Four sunflowers. It did it's job.

I have watched *Creep* a couple of days ago with my boyfriend, and oh damn, it caused me a few jumps. Funny enough there were no jumps scares, but the anticipation was enough. It really makes you think the worse until the last minute. 

## What to know going in

- It is tense and creepy (no pun intended)
- The ending keeps on giving. 
- You will think you are smarter than the main character, it's a rite of passage.

If you like the genre why not, I feel is a good addition to the other good ones out there and can give you a bit of uneasiness afterwards.
