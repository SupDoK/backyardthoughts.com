In the literal heart of Leiden, this brasserie really interested us. I went with my boyfriend for our anniversary and we took two starters, bread with a fluffy butter and salt, 2 steaks with fries, water and 2 glasses of whine. All costed 88eur, in my opinion was a good price for all the things we got. And everything had an amazing taste. They had a simple menu and dishes, which in these times I really appreciate.

## What to know

- **Make a reservation** We booked on a wednesday for 6:30pm and it was packed with big tables. I think a reservation need to be made. 
- **Wednesday and Sunday dinner deal** Apparently on Wednesday and Sunday after 5pm, there is a meal deal. One entire chicken for two people with fries and salad, at 17.95pp! We will definitely try it soon!

It is the kind of place where you want to go back to. I can't wait for the next time!
