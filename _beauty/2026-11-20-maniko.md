My god if I always hated doing my nails. The nail polish took to long to dry. The fake nails looks horrendous on me. And it just worsen my picking habit. Ah yes, I have a bad picking of my skin habit; especially around my thumb. I get to the raw skin. Not nice. 

Not matter the nail product I use, my nails have always been sharp enough that I could still pick my skin. BUT not since May this year. 
I got influenced by an ASMR artist I follow since ever: ChiaraASMR. She did a video about this brand: 
<span style="background-color: #D8BFFF; color: #FF8FB8;">
Maniko
</span>

It saved my fingers. Literally. Not only every two weeks I get to have a nice nail appointment with myself, but I also get to have such nice colours all the time. I feel liberated. 

## How it works

Basically these are little tabs of gel not fully cured, that you can easily position on your nail and then cure it under an UV light. At my first purchase, I bought the starter kit which was also on sale. And I got 3 colours, one UV light and the oil to remove them. SOOO useful. Think that since May I still have the same oil, you don't need much and it lasts so long. In the box of the individual colours, you will find 2 sets of manicures with different nails size and shape and the tools you will need to set them up. Again, so liberating. I don't have to think about much.
