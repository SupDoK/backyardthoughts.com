There is a moment every year, in which I don't know what to bring at the 5th birthday dinner and this is the time where I take this recipe to light. 

## What you need (for 12ish cupcakes)

- 125 g of butter
- 125 g of flour
- 125 g of sugar
- 2 eggs
- marmalade (or filling of your choice, yes Nutella is okay)

## What you do

1. Make the butter melt slowly in a pot while you prepare the rest. 
2. In the meantime, mix together the sugar and eggs in a bowl until smooth, either with a normal whisk or an electric one.
3. Add butter to the compost and mix all together until is creamy. 
4. Add the flour in small amounts each time, work the mixture until it's dense and creamy (better if the flour is filtered).
5. Once you feel ok with the fluffiness of the mixture take the cupcake holders and in each put 1 big spoon of the mixture. Pay attention to not use all the mixture as we will need it after. 
6. On top of each mixture you already put, add a half a tea spoon of marmalade, better if it sink in the mix.
7. With the last bit of batter remained cover each cupcake.
8. Put in oven at 180/190 C for 20/25 minutes. (check them constantly)

> For the topping, use any recipe you can find. I just mixed a bit of powdered sugar and water.

Goes very well with any function. I did it for birthdays, festivities and even bake sales.
